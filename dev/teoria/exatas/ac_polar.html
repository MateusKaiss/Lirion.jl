AC Polar

The AC model is the exact, nonlinear representation of power flow, formulated in polar coordinates, with the nodal voltages described by a magnitude $V_{b,t}$ and an angle $\theta_{b,t}$. It minimizes the generation cost subject to the network equations written without any simplification.

Derivation

Deriving the complete model requires three physical laws.

1. Complex power injection

The power flowing from a node into a branch is

\[S = V \cdot I^{*},\]

where $V$ is the phasor voltage at the node and $I$ the current leaving it into the branch.

2. Complex Ohm's law

The branch current is

\[I = Y\,(V_i - V_j),\]

where $Y$ is the series admittance connecting buses $i$ and $j$, $V_i$ is the voltage at bus $i$ and $V_j$ the voltage at bus $j$.

3. π model

A real line is not merely a series impedance: it has distributed capacitance (the line "charging"), modeled as shunt admittances $y^{fr}$, $y^{to}$ at the ends, and it may include a transformer with a complex tap on the from side. A branch $\ell$ connecting buses $i$ and $j$ and containing an ideal transformer is thus represented by:

The voltage in polar coordinates is the heart of the model:

\[V_i = v_i\,e^{j\theta_i} = v_i\,(\cos\theta_i + j\,\sin\theta_i),\]

where $v_i = |V_i|$ is the magnitude and $\theta_i$ the angle. Geometrically, it is a point in the complex plane whose projections are $v_i\cos\theta_i$ (real axis) and $v_i\sin\theta_i$ (imaginary axis):

Series admittance from $r$ and $x$

The branch has resistance $r$ and reactance $x$, hence series impedance $z = r + jx$. The admittance is its inverse:

\[y = \frac{1}{z} = \frac{1}{r + jx} = \frac{r - jx}{(r + jx)(r - jx)} = \frac{r - jx}{r^2 + x^2}.\]

Separating real and imaginary parts,

\[g = \frac{r}{r^2 + x^2}, \qquad b = -\,\frac{x}{r^2 + x^2}, \qquad y = g + jb,\]

where $g$ is the conductance, $b$ the susceptance and $y$ the series admittance.

Complex transformer tap

The transformer sits on the from end, with a complex transformation ratio

\[T = \tau\,e^{j\theta_{shift}} = \underbrace{\tau\cos\theta_{shift}}_{\tau_r} + j\,\underbrace{\tau\sin\theta_{shift}}_{\tau_i},\]

where $\tau$ is the tap magnitude and $\theta_{shift}$ the phase-shift angle. We further define

\[tm = |T|^2 = \tau^2.\]

Branch admittance matrix

Ideal transformation relation. By power conservation across the ideal transformer ($V_i\,I_i^{*} = V'\,I'^{*}$):

\[V' = \frac{V_i}{T}, \qquad I_i = \frac{I'}{T^{*}},\]

where $V'$ and $I'$ are the voltage and current at the internal node.

Kirchhoff at the internal node. From the diagram, the current entering the internal node is the one flowing through the local shunt plus the one crossing the series branch; the same holds at node $j$:

\[I' = y^{fr}\,V' + y\,(V' - V_j), \qquad I_j = y^{to}\,V_j + y\,(V_j - V').\]

Collecting $I'$ and substituting $V' = V_i/T$ into $T^{*} I_i = I'$:

\[I' = (y + y^{fr})\,V' - y\,V_j \;\Longrightarrow\; T^{*} I_i = (y + y^{fr})\,\frac{V_i}{T} - y\,V_j,\]

and isolating $I_i$ (and, analogously, $I_j$):

\[I_i = \frac{(y + y^{fr})\,V_i}{T\,T^{*}} - \frac{y}{T^{*}}\,V_j, \qquad I_j = -\frac{y}{T}\,V_i + (y + y^{to})\,V_j.\]

Identifying the matrix entries,

\[Y_{ff} = \frac{y + y^{fr}}{tm}, \qquad Y_{ft} = -\frac{y}{T^{*}}, \qquad Y_{tf} = -\frac{y}{T}, \qquad Y_{tt} = y + y^{to},\]

we obtain the branch admittance matrix:

\[\begin{bmatrix} I_i \\ I_j \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} Y_{ff} & Y_{ft} \\ Y_{tf} & Y_{tt} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} V_i \\ V_j \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \begin{aligned} I_i &= Y_{ff}\,V_i + Y_{ft}\,V_j, \\ I_j &= Y_{tf}\,V_i + Y_{tt}\,V_j. \end{aligned}\]

Power on the from side

The total power leaving bus $i$ splits into a self term and a cross term:

\[S_{ij} = V_i\,I_i^{*} = V_i\,(Y_{ff}\,V_i + Y_{ft}\,V_j)^{*} = \underbrace{V_i\,Y_{ff}^{*}\,V_i^{*}}_{\text{self term}} + \underbrace{V_i\,Y_{ft}^{*}\,V_j^{*}}_{\text{cross term}}.\]

Self term. With $V_i\,V_i^{*} = v_i^2$ and $Y_{ff}^{*} = \dfrac{(g+g^{fr}) - j(b+b^{fr})}{tm}$:

\[V_i\,Y_{ff}^{*}\,V_i^{*} = \frac{(g+g^{fr})\,v_i^2}{tm} - j\,\frac{(b+b^{fr})\,v_i^2}{tm}.\]

Cross term. With $Y_{ft}^{*} = -\dfrac{y^{*}}{T} = -\dfrac{y^{*}}{\tau}\,e^{-j\theta_{shift}}$, $V_i V_j^{*} = v_i v_j\,e^{j(\theta_i - \theta_j)}$ and introducing the effective angular aperture $\delta = \theta_i - \theta_j - \theta_{shift}$:

\[V_i\,Y_{ft}^{*}\,V_j^{*} = -\frac{y^{*}}{\tau}\,v_i\,v_j\,e^{j\delta}.\]

Expanding with $y^{*} = g - jb$ and $e^{j\delta} = \cos\delta + j\sin\delta$:

\[V_i\,Y_{ft}^{*}\,V_j^{*} = -\frac{v_i\,v_j}{\tau}\Big[(g\cos\delta + b\sin\delta) + j\,(g\sin\delta - b\cos\delta)\Big].\]

Separating the real (active) and imaginary (reactive) parts:

\[p_{ij} = \frac{(g+g^{fr})\,v_i^2}{tm} - \frac{v_i\,v_j}{\tau}\,(g\cos\delta + b\sin\delta),\]

\[q_{ij} = -\frac{(b+b^{fr})\,v_i^2}{tm} - \frac{v_i\,v_j}{\tau}\,(g\sin\delta - b\cos\delta).\]

Power on the to side

Analogously, the total power leaving bus $j$ is

\[S_{ji} = V_j\,I_j^{*} = V_j\,(Y_{tf}\,V_i + Y_{tt}\,V_j)^{*} = \underbrace{V_j\,Y_{tt}^{*}\,V_j^{*}}_{\text{self term}} + \underbrace{V_j\,Y_{tf}^{*}\,V_i^{*}}_{\text{cross term}}.\]

Self term. With $V_j\,V_j^{*} = v_j^2$ and $Y_{tt}^{*} = (g+g^{to}) - j(b+b^{to})$:

\[V_j\,Y_{tt}^{*}\,V_j^{*} = (g+g^{to})\,v_j^2 - j\,(b+b^{to})\,v_j^2.\]

Cross term. With $Y_{tf}^{*} = -\dfrac{y^{*}}{T^{*}} = -\dfrac{y^{*}}{\tau}\,e^{j\theta_{shift}}$, $V_j V_i^{*} = v_j v_i\,e^{j(\theta_j - \theta_i)}$ and $-\delta = \theta_j - \theta_i + \theta_{shift}$:

\[V_j\,Y_{tf}^{*}\,V_i^{*} = -\frac{y^{*}}{\tau}\,v_i\,v_j\,e^{-j\delta}.\]

Expanding with $y^{*} = g - jb$ and $e^{-j\delta} = \cos\delta - j\sin\delta$:

\[V_j\,Y_{tf}^{*}\,V_i^{*} = -\frac{v_i\,v_j}{\tau}\Big[(g\cos\delta - b\sin\delta) + j\,(-g\sin\delta - b\cos\delta)\Big],\]

whence

\[p_{ji} = (g+g^{to})\,v_j^2 - \frac{v_j\,v_i}{\tau}\,(g\cos\delta - b\sin\delta),\]

\[q_{ji} = -(b+b^{to})\,v_j^2 - \frac{v_i\,v_j}{\tau}\,(-g\sin\delta - b\cos\delta).\]

Main constraints

Thermal limit. The complex power on the line is $S_{ij} = P_{ij} + jQ_{ij}$, with magnitude $|S_{ij}| = \sqrt{P_{ij}^2 + Q_{ij}^2}$. The square root makes the constraint nonsmooth (not differentiable at zero and numerically more expensive). Since both sides are nonnegative, squaring preserves the inequality:

\[p_{ij}^2 + q_{ij}^2 \le rate_a^2, \qquad p_{ji}^2 + q_{ji}^2 \le rate_a^2.\]

Angle limit.

\[\theta^{\min} \le \theta_i - \theta_j \le \theta^{\max}.\]

AC Polar Formulation

Sets

  • $\mathcal{N}$: set of buses.
  • $\mathcal{N}^{nv} \subseteq \mathcal{N}$: non-virtual buses, those where the nodal balance is imposed. Virtual buses added when the network is split into zones are excluded.
  • $\mathcal{L}$: set of active branches (in-service lines and transformers).
  • $\mathcal{G}$: set of existing generators.
  • $\mathcal{G}^c \subseteq \mathcal{G}$: subset of curtailable generators (renewables).
  • $\mathcal{S}$: set of storage units.
  • $\mathcal{T} = \{1,\dots,T\}$: set of time periods in the horizon.

Indices and incidence functions

  • $b, i, j \in \mathcal{N}$: bus indices.
  • $\ell \in \mathcal{L}$: branch index.
  • $g \in \mathcal{G}$: generator index.
  • $s \in \mathcal{S}$: storage index.
  • $t \in \mathcal{T}$: time index.
  • $f(\ell),\ t(\ell)$: from and to buses of branch $\ell$.
  • $B(s)$: connection bus of storage $s$.

Parameters

Branches.

  • $g_\ell,\ b_\ell$: series conductance and susceptance of branch $\ell$.
  • $g^{fr}_\ell,\ b^{fr}_\ell$: shunt conductance and susceptance, from side.
  • $g^{to}_\ell,\ b^{to}_\ell$: shunt conductance and susceptance, to side.
  • $k_\ell$: inverse tap ratio ($1/\mathrm{tap}$), the derivation's $1/\tau$, so $k_\ell^2 = 1/tm$.
  • $\Phi_\ell$: transformer phase shift, the derivation's $\theta_{shift}$.
  • $\overline{S}_\ell$: apparent-flow limit of the branch.
  • $\delta_\ell^{\min},\ \delta_\ell^{\max}$: angular aperture limits of the branch.

Buses.

  • $V_b^{\min},\ V_b^{\max}$: voltage magnitude limits at bus $b$.
  • $g^{sh}_b,\ b^{sh}_b$: shunt conductance and susceptance of the bus.
  • $P^d_{b,t},\ Q^d_{b,t}$: active and reactive demand at bus $b$.

Generators.

  • $P_g^{\min},\ P_g^{\max}$: static active-power limits.
  • $\overline{P}^{ts}_{g,t},\ \underline{P}^{ts}_{g,t}$: active-power time envelope.
  • $Q_g^{\min},\ Q_g^{\max}$: static reactive-power limits.
  • $\overline{Q}^{ts}_{g,t},\ \underline{Q}^{ts}_{g,t}$: reactive-power time envelope.
  • $c_{g,t,k}$: coefficients of the cost polynomial of $g$.
  • $c^{curt}_g$: unit curtailment cost of $g$.

Storage.

  • $E_s^{\min},\ E_s^{\max}$: stored-energy limits.
  • $E^0_s$: initial energy of unit $s$.
  • $C_s^{\max},\ D_s^{\max}$: maximum charge and discharge power.
  • $\eta^c_s,\ \eta^d_s$: charge and discharge efficiencies.
  • $r_s,\ x_s$: converter resistance and reactance.
  • $P^{loss}_s,\ Q^{loss}_s$: fixed active and reactive losses.
  • $\overline{S}_s$: inverter thermal limit.
  • $Q_s^{\min},\ Q_s^{\max}$: reactive-power limits of the storage.

Decision variables

Voltage.

\[\theta_{b,t} \in \mathbb{R} \;\text{: voltage angle at bus } b \text{ at time } t. \tag{1}\]

\[V_{b,t} \in [V_b^{\min}, V_b^{\max}] \;\text{: voltage magnitude at bus } b. \tag{2}\]

Generation.

\[p^g_{g,t} \in [P_g^{\min}, P_g^{\max}] \;\text{: active power generated by } g. \tag{3}\]

\[Q^g_{g,t} \in [Q_g^{\min}, Q_g^{\max}] \;\text{: reactive power generated by } g. \tag{4}\]

\[p^c_{g,t} \in [0, \overline{P}^{ts}_{g,t}] \;\text{: curtailed renewable power, } g \in \mathcal{G}^c. \tag{5}\]

Storage.

\[E_{s,t} \in [E_s^{\min}, E_s^{\max}] \;\text{: energy stored in } s \text{ at the end of } t. \tag{6}\]

\[c_{s,t} \in [0, C_s^{\max}] \;\text{: charging power of } s. \tag{7}\]

\[d_{s,t} \in [0, D_s^{\max}] \;\text{: discharging power of } s. \tag{8}\]

\[P^s_{s,t} \in [-\overline{S}_s, \overline{S}_s] \;\text{: net active power exchanged by } s \text{, positive when charging (drawn from the grid).} \tag{9}\]

\[Q^s_{s,t} \in [Q_s^{\min}, Q_s^{\max}] \;\text{: net reactive power exchanged by } s \text{, positive when absorbed from the grid.} \tag{10}\]

\[Q^{sc}_{s,t} \in [Q_s^{\min}, Q_s^{\max}] \;\text{: internal reactive compensation of the converter of } s. \tag{11}\]

Objective function

The fuel cost is evaluated on the power in physical units ($S^{base}\,p^g$), with the polynomial written in decreasing order of degree: for a vector of $m_{g,t}$ coefficients, term $k$ has exponent $m_{g,t} - k$.

\[F = \sum_{t\in\mathcal{T}} \sum_{g\in\mathcal{G}} \sum_{k=1}^{m_{g,t}} c_{g,t,k}\left(S^{base}\,p^g_{g,t}\right)^{m_{g,t} - k}. \tag{12}\]

The curtailment cost, when curtailable generators are present:

\[F^{curt} = \sum_{g\in\mathcal{G}^c} \sum_{t\in\mathcal{T}} c^{curt}_g\,S^{base}\,p^c_{g,t}. \tag{13}\]

Hence:

\[\min\; F + F^{curt}. \tag{14}\]

Branch flows

Define the branch effective angular aperture, with the transformer phase shift already subtracted:

\[\delta_{\ell,t} = \theta_{f(\ell),t} - \theta_{t(\ell),t} - \Phi_\ell, \qquad \ell\in\mathcal{L},\ t\in\mathcal{T}. \tag{15}\]

The four flows (active and reactive, in each direction) are:

\[P^f_{\ell,t} = (g_\ell + g^{fr}_\ell)\,(k_\ell\,V_{f(\ell),t})^2 - k_\ell\,V_{f(\ell),t}\,V_{t(\ell),t}\, (g_\ell\cos\delta_{\ell,t} + b_\ell\sin\delta_{\ell,t}). \tag{16}\]

\[P^t_{\ell,t} = (g_\ell + g^{to}_\ell)\,V_{t(\ell),t}^2 - k_\ell\,V_{t(\ell),t}\,V_{f(\ell),t}\, (g_\ell\cos\delta_{\ell,t} - b_\ell\sin\delta_{\ell,t}). \tag{17}\]

\[Q^f_{\ell,t} = -(b_\ell + b^{fr}_\ell)\,(k_\ell\,V_{f(\ell),t})^2 - k_\ell\,V_{f(\ell),t}\,V_{t(\ell),t}\, (-b_\ell\cos\delta_{\ell,t} + g_\ell\sin\delta_{\ell,t}). \tag{18}\]

\[Q^t_{\ell,t} = -(b_\ell + b^{to}_\ell)\,V_{t(\ell),t}^2 - k_\ell\,V_{t(\ell),t}\,V_{f(\ell),t}\, (-b_\ell\cos\delta_{\ell,t} - g_\ell\sin\delta_{\ell,t}). \tag{19}\]

Power balance

The physical idea is simple and inviolable: at each bus, everything that comes in must go out.

Active power.

\[\sum_{g\in\mathcal{G}(b)} p^g_{g,t} - \sum_{s\in\mathcal{S}(b)} P^s_{s,t} - P^d_{b,t} - \sum_{\ell:\,f(\ell)=b} P^f_{\ell,t} - \sum_{\ell:\,t(\ell)=b} P^t_{\ell,t} - g^{sh}_b\,V_{b,t}^2 = 0, \qquad b\in\mathcal{N}^{nv},\ t\in\mathcal{T}. \tag{20}\]

Reactive power.

\[\sum_{g\in\mathcal{G}(b)} Q^g_{g,t} - \sum_{s\in\mathcal{S}(b)} Q^s_{s,t} - Q^d_{b,t} - \sum_{\ell:\,f(\ell)=b} Q^f_{\ell,t} - \sum_{\ell:\,t(\ell)=b} Q^t_{\ell,t} + b^{sh}_b\,V_{b,t}^2 = 0, \qquad b\in\mathcal{N}^{nv},\ t\in\mathcal{T}. \tag{21}\]

Branch thermal limits

\[(P^f_{\ell,t})^2 + (Q^f_{\ell,t})^2 \le \overline{S}_\ell^2, \qquad \ell\in\mathcal{L},\ t\in\mathcal{T}. \tag{22}\]

\[(P^t_{\ell,t})^2 + (Q^t_{\ell,t})^2 \le \overline{S}_\ell^2, \qquad \ell\in\mathcal{L},\ t\in\mathcal{T}. \tag{23}\]

Angular aperture limits

Imposed on the raw difference $\theta_f - \theta_t$ (without $\Phi_\ell$):

\[\delta_\ell^{\min} \le \theta_{f(\ell),t} - \theta_{t(\ell),t} \le \delta_\ell^{\max}, \qquad \ell\in\mathcal{L},\ t\in\mathcal{T}. \tag{24}\]

Reference bus

\[\theta_{ref,t} = 0, \qquad t\in\mathcal{T}. \tag{25}\]

Generation constraints

Time envelope; added only when the period limit $\overline{P}^{ts}_{g,t}$ is below $P_g^{\max}$.

\[p^g_{g,t} \le \overline{P}^{ts}_{g,t}, \qquad g\in\mathcal{G}\setminus\mathcal{G}^c,\ t\in\mathcal{T}. \tag{26}\]

\[p^g_{g,t} \ge \underline{P}^{ts}_{g,t}, \qquad g\in\mathcal{G},\ t\in\mathcal{T}. \tag{27}\]

\[\underline{Q}^{ts}_{g,t} \le Q^g_{g,t} \le \overline{Q}^{ts}_{g,t}, \qquad g\in\mathcal{G},\ t\in\mathcal{T}. \tag{28}\]

Renewable generation curtailment.

\[p^g_{g,t} + p^c_{g,t} = \overline{P}^{ts}_{g,t}, \qquad g\in\mathcal{G}^c,\ t\in\mathcal{T}. \tag{29}\]

Storage constraints

Energy balance.

\[E_{s,1} = E^0_s + \eta^c_s\,c_{s,1} - \frac{d_{s,1}}{\eta^d_s}, \qquad s\in\mathcal{S}. \tag{30}\]

\[E_{s,t} = E_{s,t-1} + \eta^c_s\,c_{s,t} - \frac{d_{s,t}}{\eta^d_s}, \qquad s\in\mathcal{S},\ t = 2,\dots,T. \tag{31}\]

Non-simultaneity of charging and discharging.

\[c_{s,t}\,d_{s,t} = 0, \qquad s\in\mathcal{S},\ t\in\mathcal{T}. \tag{32}\]

Inverter thermal limit.

\[(P^s_{s,t})^2 + (Q^s_{s,t})^2 \le \overline{S}_s^2, \qquad s\in\mathcal{S},\ t\in\mathcal{T}. \tag{33}\]

Converter losses (AC coupling). The ohmic loss is proportional to the square of the current, $|S|^2/V^2$, evaluated at the connection bus $B(s)$:

\[P^s_{s,t} + d_{s,t} - c_{s,t} = P^{loss}_s + r_s\,\frac{(P^s_{s,t})^2 + (Q^s_{s,t})^2}{V_{B(s),t}^2}, \qquad s\in\mathcal{S},\ t\in\mathcal{T}. \tag{34}\]

\[Q^s_{s,t} = Q^{sc}_{s,t} + Q^{loss}_s + x_s\,\frac{(P^s_{s,t})^2 + (Q^s_{s,t})^2}{V_{B(s),t}^2}, \qquad s\in\mathcal{S},\ t\in\mathcal{T}. \tag{35}\]

Complete model

\[\min_{\substack{\theta,\,V,\,p^g,\,Q^g,\,p^c,\\ E,\,c,\,d,\,P^s,\,Q^s,\,Q^{sc}}} \quad F + F^{curt} \qquad (\text{Eq. } 14)\]
