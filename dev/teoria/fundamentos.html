Foundations

The theory pages each stand on their own, but they all share one network model and one set of conventions. This page collects them, so the formulation pages do not repeat the groundwork.

Sets and indices

SymbolMeaning
$\mathcal{N}$buses; $\mathcal{N}^{nv}\subseteq\mathcal{N}$ the non-virtual buses where the power balance is enforced
$\mathcal{L}$active branches; $f(\ell),\ t(\ell)$ the from and to buses of branch $\ell$
$\mathcal{G}$generators; $\mathcal{G}(b)$ those at bus $b$; $\mathcal{G}^c$ the curtailable (renewable) ones
$\mathcal{S}$storage units; $\mathcal{S}(b)$ those at bus $b$
$\mathcal{T}=\{1,\dots,T\}$time periods

A virtual bus carries no load, generation, or shunt. It exists only to glue zones together in a distributed solve, so the nodal balance skips it, which is what $\mathcal{N}^{nv}$ selects.

The branch model

Every branch is a $\pi$-section behind an ideal complex transformer. From the resistance $r_\ell$ and reactance $x_\ell$ the series admittance is

\[y_\ell = g_\ell + j\,b_\ell,\qquad g_\ell = \frac{r_\ell}{r_\ell^2 + x_\ell^2},\qquad b_\ell = \frac{-x_\ell}{r_\ell^2 + x_\ell^2}.\]

The line charging sits at the two ends as shunt admittances $y^{fr}_\ell = g^{fr}_\ell + j\,b^{fr}_\ell$ and $y^{to}_\ell = g^{to}_\ell + j\,b^{to}_\ell$. A plain line splits its total charging in half, $b^{fr}_\ell = b^{to}_\ell = b^{sh}_\ell/2$ with $g^{fr}_\ell = g^{to}_\ell = 0$. The transformer is a complex tap on the from side,

\[T_\ell = \tau_\ell\, e^{j\theta^{shift}_\ell},\qquad tm_\ell = |T_\ell|^2 = \tau_\ell^2,\]

with tap ratio $\tau_\ell$ and phase shift $\theta^{shift}_\ell$. A branch with no transformer has $\tau_\ell = 1$ and $\theta^{shift}_\ell = 0$.

The branch admittance block

Put together, a branch ties its two terminal currents to its two terminal voltages through a $2\times 2$ block,

\[\begin{bmatrix} I_f \\ I_t \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} Y_{ff} & Y_{ft} \\ Y_{tf} & Y_{tt} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} V_f \\ V_t \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \begin{aligned} Y_{ff} &= \frac{y_\ell + y^{fr}_\ell}{tm_\ell}, &\quad Y_{ft} &= -\frac{y_\ell}{\overline{T_\ell}}, \\[2pt] Y_{tf} &= -\frac{y_\ell}{T_\ell}, &\quad Y_{tt} &= y_\ell + y^{to}_\ell. \end{aligned}\]

This block is the single object every formulation starts from. The AC pages expand it into real and reactive flows, the W-space relaxations lift the voltage products $V_i\,\overline{V_j}$, and the branch-flow models rewrite it around the squared current magnitude. On a plain line ($T_\ell = 1$) it collapses to the textbook $\pi$-model, $Y_{ff} = y_\ell + y^{fr}_\ell$, $Y_{ft} = Y_{tf} = -y_\ell$, $Y_{tt} = y_\ell + y^{to}_\ell$.

Conventions

  • Orientation. Flows are measured at each end: $P^f_\ell, Q^f_\ell$ at the from bus and $P^t_\ell, Q^t_\ell$ at the to bus. Losses make $P^f_\ell \neq -P^t_\ell$ in general.
  • Nodal balance. At every non-virtual bus, generation minus storage minus load minus the flows leaving the bus minus the bus shunt $y^{sh}_b|V_b|^2$ is zero.
  • Voltage state. Polar models carry $V_b$ and $\theta_b$; rectangular models carry $v^{re}_b, v^{im}_b$; the W-space relaxations carry the squared magnitude $w_b = |V_b|^2$ and the bus-pair products. One bus is pinned as the angle reference, $\theta_{ref} = 0$.
  • Per unit. Everything is in per unit on the system base, so impedances, powers, and voltages are dimensionless and the relations above carry no scaling constants.
